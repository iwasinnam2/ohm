Shadow agents, repeat spend, unsanctioned browse, and opaque FinOps — the chaos that shows up after the first successful demo. withOhm is the chaos governor around a metered exact-replay pipe.

## Summary

- **SSO & org policy** — Who may cross the pipe
- **Clean ledger** — Cost centers, export, spend caps on MISS flood
- **Compliant fetch** — Public-web ingest with intent
- **Exact-replay** — Identical work stops re-buying upstream tokens
- **Honesty** — Published non-goals so marketing cannot outrun the system

## Why this is a solution page

Enterprises do not need another chatbot. They need **governable** mechanical AI traffic: same OpenAI shape developers already use, with meters and receipts finance can read.

## Anti-pattern: sprawl of keys and tabs

Personal API keys, unmanaged Cursor configs, scrapers without policy — spend and risk without a crossing you can audit.
